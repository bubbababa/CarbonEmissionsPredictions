Project topic and timeline is clear. However, your goal isn't as clear. Are you trying to predict carbon intensity based on factors like weather, power demand, and dispatch order? If so, to forecast carbon intensity ahead of time, you have to forecast those other factors as well. If my understanding is correct, carbon intensity is calculated based on what type of resource is currently being used to generate electricity so it essentially becomes a function of resources being used at a given time. To be able to forecast that, you need to be able to forecast the resources that will be used which will require you to forecast the factors that determine which resource(s) will be used. If the data you collect from U.S. Energy Information Administration provides that, great, but if not, that might be something you need to keep in mind. To be able to have the temporal resolution you're looking for (days to hours ahead of time) might be a little ambitious because forecasting on that scale is a very difficult task to do accurately, especially with noisy data, but if you think your data will provide you that level of temporal resolution feel free to go with that. It might be more reasonable to predict how carbon intensity will change over the period of weeks to months.
